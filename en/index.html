The flat

Welcome to a very attractive top floor flat in the popular La Dama area. If you want a holiday in a central spot by the Mediterranean, in an area that is lively all year round, this could be the place for you.

Everything is on one level: two bedrooms, one bathroom, a kitchen, a living room and a spacious balcony of about 20 m². The living room opens straight onto the west facing balcony, which gets good sun from late morning onwards. The balcony feels airy and has a view over the surroundings.

The award winning Aldea del Mar development, with its well kept gardens, gives the building green and tidy surroundings. The shared area has a swimming pool, an outdoor shower, benches and planting.

Area

Playa de Los Locos is just two kilometres north east of Torrevieja town centre and about 45 minutes from Alicante International Airport.

From the flat it is only a two minute walk to the sandy beach at Playa de Los Locos, restaurants, a bus stop and the promenade that leads into town. The area is urban, yet it feels calm, green and sheltered. There is a large supermarket nearby, and the promenade and side streets have small shops and other services. Most things are within walking distance, so you can easily manage without a hire car.

About Torrevieja

Torrevieja is a lively coastal town on the Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante. It has grown from a traditional fishing and salt town into one of the most popular destinations in the region, while keeping its Spanish character and an active local community all year round.

A mild microclimate
The town is surrounded by two large salt lakes, Las Salinas, which give it a mild and pleasant microclimate. The area has more than 320 days of sunshine a year, mild winters and pleasant sea breezes in summer.
Beaches and promenade
A promenade several kilometres long runs past restaurants, cafés and shops. As well as Playa de Los Locos, the town has well known sandy beaches such as Playa del Cura in the centre and the long Playa de La Mata to the north.
Things to do
Large shopping centres such as Habaneras and Zenia Boulevard, a hospital, weekly markets, water parks and plenty of culture. Several international golf courses, such as Villamartin, Las Ramblas and Campoamor, are a short drive away.
Easy to get to
An airport bus runs regularly from Alicante airport straight to the bus station in Torrevieja town centre. From the bus station it is a 20 minute walk to the flat.

If you love a beautiful sunrise, the sandy beach at Playa de Los Locos is a lovely place to start the day.
